518. Vietnam Medical Journal
   └── The Vietnam Medical Journal was established in 1955 and has been continuously published for 72 years since its first issue in September 1955. Initially, it published full texts in Vietnamese (with French and Russian summaries); in later years, especially since 1990, French and English summaries have been added due to increased English proficiency and limited Russian reading capacity. The content consists of medical research papers presented at domestic and international scientific conferences (no commercial advertising), published twice a month (24 issues per year), with a print run of 1000 copies. On February 12, 2024, the Ministry of Information and Communications issued document No. 555/BTTT-CBC approving the change in the number of pages and publication frequency of the Vietnam Medical Journal to: 380 pages; publication frequency of 3 issues per month. The Vietnam Medical Journal is considered one of the most prestigious scientific journals in the field of healthcare in Vietnam, with the State Council for Professor Titles awarding a maximum of 1 point per article.
